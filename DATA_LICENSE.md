# Data licence

Aggregated data produced by this project (normalised observations, derived series, exports) is released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

It is derived from upstream sources that keep their own terms:

- **data.gov.sg** datasets (ED waiting times, NEA weather and air quality, LTA taxi availability, MOM public holidays, MOH ICU utilisation):
  Singapore Open Data Licence v1.0. Contains information from data.gov.sg accessed on the dates recorded in the run log.
- **Hong Kong Hospital Authority** A&E waiting times: used for method development and benchmarking only, not redistributed.

Raw upstream payloads are not part of the CC BY release unless the upstream licence allows it.
